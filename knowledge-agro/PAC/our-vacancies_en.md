The open vacancies of the Directorate-General for Agriculture and Rural Development are published below and on the Job Opportunities page of the EPSO portal.
Candidates interested in applying for published contract staff positions must be registered in the European Personnel Selection Office (EPSO) database and be included in CAST Permanent in the grade and requested profile (mentioned in the vacancy notice) for which they are applying.

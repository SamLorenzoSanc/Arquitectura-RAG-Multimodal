Strengthening the position of farmers in the food supply chain
Reinforcing the position of farmers in the food supply chain is one of the key objectives of the CAP. The degree of trust and cooperation between actors in the chain is increasing thanks to several measures already in place at EU level.
While the full implementation and enforcement of the available policy tools take time, more is already under preparation: EU actions to address farmers’ concerns.

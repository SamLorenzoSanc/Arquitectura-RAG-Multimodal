Shape the future of agriculture unlocking digital innovation
From reducing administrative burden to improving sustainability and strengthening transparency across the agri-food chain, innovative and digital solutions can make a real difference for Europe’s farming community.
From 16 to 18 October 2026, the EU Agri-Hackathon will connect participants with top experts in agriculture, technology and research as well as business leaders and policymakers from all over Europe.
Are you a farmer, data analyst, developer, researcher, entrepreneur, student, agri-food professional or policy expert from the EU?
Sign up for the first ever EU Agri-Hackathon and develop practical and forward-looking digital tools to support the farmers of the future responding to their concrete needs.
The challenges
Your valuable skills are needed to contribute to a more sustainable, resilient and innovative agricultural sector in Europe.
All three challenges share one ambition: simplification. EU farmers today face growing complexity, in administrative obligations, in the data their operations generate, and in the information demands placed on them across the food chain.
This is your chance to build digital solutions that cut through that complexity and give farmers back time, clarity and control.
- Challenge #1: Less time on forms, more time on farmsGoal: help farmers save time and improve efficiency. How? Be the one developing the right digital tools by transforming manual administrative tasks into simplified and automated reporting systems, reducing the bureaucratic burden.
- Challenge #2: Connect data for smarter fieldsGoal: help farmers simplify how they manage and use the data their farm generates. How? Build solutions that help farmers deal with the flood of data generated by sensors, connected machines and digital devices on their farm and turn it into clear, actionable information for everyday decisions.
- Challenge #3: Flowing data, empowered collaborationGoal: help farmers simplify how they share information and engage with value chain partners. How? Develop solutions that streamline the flow of information between farmers and their value chain partners, reducing the reporting burden, ensuring farmers retain control of their data, and strengthening their position across the food chain.
Why apply?
- Teams will benefit from mentoring sessions, exchanges with specialists and opportunities to refine their ideas throughout the event.
- The developers of the winning project will be invited to an award ceremony as part of the EU Agri-Food Days conference, the yearly flagship conference in the field of agriculture in the EU gathering more than 1 000 people each year. The winning idea will be showcased during the award ceremony, which will offer visibility on European communication channels, presence with a booth at the EU Agri-Food Days partners exhibition providing great networking opportunities to grow ideas.
- A special mention will be awarded to the best solutions in the categories of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Open-Source development.
Details about additional benefits will be announced at a later date!
Who can apply?
Who are we looking for?
We are looking for farmers, data analysts, developers, researchers, entrepreneurs, students, agri-food professionals and policy experts who have the following profile.
- Agriculture and Environment Studies:- Agronomy/Agri-economics
- Environmental science/Engineering
- EU agricultural policy
- Technology and Data:- Data science
- Computer science/Engineering
- Software development
- Economics:- Business strategy
- Statistics
Eligibility Criteria
To be eligible, you must meet all three of the following conditions:
- Be a legal resident of an EU country
- Be 18 years of age or older at the time of registration
- Be sufficiently fluent in English to participate in all sessions, mentoring, and to submit your project
The competing teams will own the Intellectual Property (IP) rights of the solutions developed during the EU Agri-Hackathon.
How to apply?
To apply, fill out our registration form.
You can apply as:
- an individual or
- as part of a pre-formed team.
Applicants from a wide range of backgrounds are welcome.
If you apply as an individual, you will be assigned to a team. You will meet your team members and all participants during a virtual team-building session ahead of the event.
If you apply as part of a team, it is recommended to combine different types of knowledge and experience, as this will be taken into account in the selection of the participants in the Hackathon.
Places are limited and participants will be selected based on their profile, motivation and the diversity of skills and experience.
Find out more in the Applicants kit.

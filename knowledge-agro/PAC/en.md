The PAC Network
The CAP Network is the meeting point for all individuals and entities related to rural areas and agricultural activity, with the aim of disseminating and ensuring compliance with the objectives of Spain's CAP Strategic Plan.
At the same time, it aims to raise awareness among the population about the importance of rural areas and the agricultural sector for our present and future.
Our Themes
We gather all the information related to the major issues that concern and motivate the rural world.
Subscribe to our regular publications
In its monthly newsletter, the CAP Network aims to disseminate the activities carried out by CAP Network members and Local Action Groups, the European CAP Network, as well as those organized by the network's own technical team.
Its quarterly magazine, Savia Rural, covers current topics in rural and agricultural development and features reports on inspiring projects, with innovation and sustainability as its core themes.

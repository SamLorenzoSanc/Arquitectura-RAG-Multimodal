Since 1962, the common agricultural policy (CAP) has been a cornerstone in enhancing agricultural productivity, supporting farmers' livelihoods, and ensuring a stable supply of affordable food for European citizens.
The CAP remains one of the widely known and accepted EU common policies according to the latest Eurobarometer.
As a vital policy not only for farmers but for the entire European population, it is essential to clarify certain aspects of the CAP that are more susceptible to misconceptions or targeted by information manipulation campaigns, which aim to distort facts, misrepresent motives, and erode trust in EU institutions.

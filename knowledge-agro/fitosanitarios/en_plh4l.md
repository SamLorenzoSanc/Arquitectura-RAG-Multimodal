Plant health for life.
Welcome to #PlantHealth4Life, an EU campaign that promotes the vital link between plant health and our everyday lives. Plant pests and diseases can impact our food security, the economy, and the environment, while healthy plants mean healthy food and a sustainable future. We all have a role to play to keep plants healthy. Join #PlantHealth4Life and find out what you can do to improve plant health and protect your community.
Why plant health matters
Plants make up 80% of the food we eat. They clean the air we breathe and help us fight climate change by reducing carbon from the atmosphere. When plants are healthy, they provide habitats and food sources for numerous animal species, contributing to biodiversity and the overall stability of our ecosystem. By protecting plants we are protecting life.
The role of plants
Plants play a vital role in our environment, ensuring food security, and paving the way for a sustainable future.
Economic impact
Plant health is not only vital for our environment and food security, it also plays a significant role in shaping our economy.
Environmental impact
Plant health plays a crucial role in maintaining a balanced and sustainable environment. When plants are healthy, they provide numerous benefits.
Threats to plant health
By preventing threats to plant health, we can ensure a sustainable future for ourselves, our communities, and the environment.

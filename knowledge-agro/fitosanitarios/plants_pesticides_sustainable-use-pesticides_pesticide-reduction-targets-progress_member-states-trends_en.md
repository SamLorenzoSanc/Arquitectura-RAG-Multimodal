The European Commission has calculated, using the methodology explained on these webpages, the progress made by each Member State towards meeting the two non legally binding pesticide reduction targets.
The Commission has provided each Member State with this information, and encouraged them to make this information public.
Where Member States have agreed for the Commission to publish this progress, this information is available below.

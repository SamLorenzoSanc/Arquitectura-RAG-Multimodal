Animal health requirements for movement between Member States and entry into the Union of semen, oocytes and embryos have been harmonised.
This has been carried out to guarantee the absence of specific pathogens that might be transmitted by semen, oocytes and embryos and to avoid infection of female recipients and their offspring.

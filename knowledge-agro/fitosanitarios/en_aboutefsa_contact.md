Contact us
Specific enquiries
EFSA welcomes feedback, and we answer questions from any party interested in our work. All questions receive an individual response. We invite you to read our Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) before sending a query. If you do not find a response to your question in the FAQ, you can send us a request through the Ask a Question service.
Ask a Question
If you have a question about EFSA, applications for regulated products, careers, public access to documents (PAD), or other generic enquiries, please check our FAQs or use the Ask a Question service.
Complaints
If you wish to register a complaint about EFSA, please consult our complaints guide.

Agronomists, biologists, chemists, entomologists… the list of scientists working at EFSA is long and diverse. Not all of them are staff members. Scientific experts may join EFSA also as members of our scientific panels or as guest researchers for specific projects. And our Seconded National Experts help strengthen the links between EFSA and our national partner organisations.
Contribute to our scientific work
As a scientist at EFSA you will play an active role in an organisation that makes a difference to the lives of 500 million EU citizens.
Experts
As an expert, you can join our Scientific Committee, one of our 11 scientific panels or their working groups. The panels are renewed every 5 years via dedicated calls for experts.
As a panel or working group expert, you will contribute to the large body of scientific work EFSA produces every year.
Seconded National Experts & Guest Scientists
As a guest scientist, you will bring your expertise to specific projects that EFSA is working on for a period of between 6 to 12 months.
As a Seconded National Expert (SNE) you will join us for between 6 months to 4 years and take back the expertise you have gained at EFSA to your home administration once your assignment with us has finished.
As a panel or working group expert, you will contribute to the large body of scientific work EFSA produces every year.
Scientific staff
Our staff in Parma is diverse and international. Our colleagues hail from all EU countries and bring with them different backgrounds and experiences.
Science is at the core of our work, and we are always looking for specialists to join our team.

About the group
The aim of this group is to:
- Advise the Commission on issues relating to animal health legislation, its implementation and policy development
- Exchange information, experience and good practice on animal health covered by EU legislation
Its tasks are:
- Assist the Commission in defining policy and preparing legislation or delegated acts
- Give expertise on implementing EU legislation, programmes and policies, on coordination with EU countries and stakeholders
See Register of Commission expert groups and other similar entities for information on this group (code E00930).

Newsletters
DG Health and Food Safety (DG SANTE) electronic publications include:
Health and Food Safety Newsletter
A thematic selection of news and activities in the field of Public Health and Food Safety, published twice per month and available in all EU official languages. New features and sections were introduced in 2020 - Subscribe
EU Food Loss and Waste Prevention Hub
Stay up to date with the latest news on food loss and waste prevention actions taken across the EU! Subscribe to the Platform's newsletter to receive monthly news on initiatives carried out by its members: Subscribe
eNews
Email updates on the latest news and developments from DG Health and Food Safety, tailored to your interests. You may subscribe to one or more topics:
